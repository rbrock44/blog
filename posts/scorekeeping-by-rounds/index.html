Why I Built Scorekeeping by Rounds

August 17, 2026·1 min read

  • angular
  • side-projects

It started because I had a cousin pass away, and so his parents — my aunt and uncle — decided to do organ donation. They got involved with Saint Louis Transplant of America, and at the yearly trivia game I inquired about how to change them from using Excel. I found the format to be a very poor user experience from the audience's perspective.

The lady in charge said it would have to be some free tool, and at the time I was working a job that had 10% learning time. The tech stack there used Angular, so I decided to practice my skills and make a website. I completed this project, and adoption turned out harder than I thought.

To fundraise for his scholarship, my aunt and uncle held a disc golf tournament in his honor. They did that for several years, then migrated to a local trivia night they host every year. My niece has used this as scorekeeper every year, providing a great experience. She can edit scores on one monitor and have the scores display on the monitor projected to the audience.
